Run Chef - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Run Chef (package com.runchef.game) is a mobile game published by griyazilim ("we", "us").

Data we collect

Run Chef does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The game has no user accounts, contains no advertising or analytics services, and does not request internet access, so it works completely offline.

Permissions

The game uses only the device vibration permission, to give haptic feedback during play. No other permission is requested.

Third parties

We do not share any information with third parties. Google Play may process installs and crash reports according to Google's own privacy policy.

Children

Run Chef is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
